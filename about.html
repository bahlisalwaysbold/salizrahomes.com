About Salizra Homes

Built by engineers
who read drawings

Founder & MD/CEO

Engr. Salisu Sumaila

Engr. Salisu Sumaila is the Founder and Managing Director/CEO of Salizra Homes Limited. With a background in civil engineering, he leads the company's approach to real estate development, building construction and civil engineering works, overseeing every project from the earliest structural drawing through to final handover. His focus is on getting the technical detail right first, so that what gets built on site matches exactly what was designed on paper.

Under his leadership, Salizra Homes Limited (RC 1736628) has taken on residential, commercial and civil engineering projects across Niger State, supported by a team of site engineers and project staff who keep the company close to every site it works on.

Our Story

From structural drawing
to standing structure.

Salizra Homes Limited was founded to close the gap between design and delivery — between a well-prepared structural drawing and a well-built structure. Registered as RC 1736628 and based in Tafa, Niger State, the company works across real estate, building construction and civil engineering, taking on projects of every size with the same level of technical care.

◎
Our Mission

To deliver real estate, building and civil engineering projects that are structurally sound, on schedule, and built to the specification agreed with every client.

◈
Our Vision

To become a trusted name in real estate and civil engineering across Niger State and beyond, known for precision from the drawing board to the finished site.
