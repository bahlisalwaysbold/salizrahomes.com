Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from clients before we start a project.

How much does it cost to build a duplex in Niger State?
Typical duplex costs in Niger State vary by plot size, finish level, and site conditions. A practical range is usually discussed after we review your drawings, budget, and exact location.
Do I need my own structural drawing before I contact you?
Not necessarily. If you already have drawings, we can review them and work from them. If you do not, we can help with planning and the structural detailing needed for execution on site.
Do you handle small projects like fencing or single-room extensions?
Yes. We handle projects of different sizes with the same engineering discipline, from small boundary fencing and site works to larger residential and commercial builds.
How long does a typical residential project take?
The timeline depends on the size of the project, weather, material delivery, and site conditions. We usually guide clients with a realistic programme after the initial consultation and site review.
What areas do you work in?
We are based in Tafa, Niger State, and work across Niger State and neighbouring locations depending on the project requirements and client needs.
How do I get started?
Send us your project details, drawings, or site information and we will respond with the next step, including a consultation or site visit if needed.
