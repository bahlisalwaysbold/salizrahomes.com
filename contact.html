Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions.

How much does it cost to build a duplex in Abuja?

Cost varies significantly based on location, size, finishes, soil conditions and design complexity. A standard 4–5 bedroom duplex in Abuja typically ranges from ₦40M–₦120M+. Share your location, design preferences and budget range for a more useful estimate.

What areas do you work in?

We are based at Peace Plaza, Utako, Abuja, and undertake projects across Abuja and other states in Nigeria, including recent work in Akwa Ibom and Kogi States.

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. Our integrated structure covers structural detailing, building construction and civil engineering — so the same team that produces the drawings also coordinates the site execution.

What do I need to start a conversation?

Location, project type (new build, renovation, road, structural drawings), any drawings or reference images you have, budget range and preferred timing. The project brief form on this page formats that into a draft email for you.
